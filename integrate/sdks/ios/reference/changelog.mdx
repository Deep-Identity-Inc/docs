The full notes are in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Deep-Identity-Inc/deepidv-sdk-swift/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) on GitHub. The SDK follows semantic versioning; minor releases can change defaults, listed under **Upgrading**.

<Update label="1.3.0" description="November 2026">
  A redesigned capture experience: recognized ID cards and passports, one instruction at a time, brand styling, English, Spanish and French, and ID photos cut to the capture frame.

  **Upgrading from 1.2** — see [Migrating to 1.3](/integrate/sdks/ios/reference/migrating-to-1-3)
  - `documentCamera` defaults to `.front`. Phones should pass `.back`.
  - `showsDocumentCaptureIntro` defaults to `false`.
  - A submitted workflow run ends on a thank-you screen before `onResult` (`showsCompletionScreen`).
  - `sessionStatus` reads `.verified` / `.rejected` where it read `AUTO_APPROVE` / `DECLINED`.
  - The re-verification view has its own close button.
  - Proxies must forward `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/id-capture/detect`.

  **Added**
  - Document recognition during capture in workflows: the document's name on the frame, an upside-down check, health cards never auto-captured, and "This looks like…" for the wrong document.
  - Passports auto-capture, with a passport-shaped frame.
  - Turn-to-landscape guidance for cards held sideways.
  - Redesigned document, selfie and liveness screens, and shared wait, result and error screens, in light and dark.
  - The closing thank-you screen.
  - `.deepIDVAppearance(_:)` for colors, fonts, corner radius and appearance.
  - English, Spanish and French, and `DeepIDVConfig.language`.
  - `documentCamera:` on `DeepIDVWorkflowView`, `DeepIDVVerificationView` and `DocumentScannerView`.
  - Center Stage is turned off during document capture.

  **Changed**
  - ID photos are taken at up to 12 MP and cut to the frame on screen.
  - Two extra frames of each card front and passport page go with the ID step, to recover an unreadable expiry date or date of birth.
  - The document picker is skipped when the workflow accepts only one document type.
  - US spelling on screen, except official document names.
  - The submitting screen no longer offers Cancel while photos are being sent.
</Update>

<Update label="1.2.1" description="October 2026">
  - `WorkflowUser.phone` is optional (`String?`, default `nil`).
</Update>

<Update label="1.2.0" description="October 2026">
  - **Breaking:** `makeReVerifyView(workflowID:onResult:)` is replaced by `makeReVerifyView(workflowID:email:onResult:)`.
  - Added `NotEligibleReason.userNotFound` and `.notPreviouslyVerified`.
</Update>

<Update label="1.1.0" description="October 2026">
  - Typed workflow failures: `WorkflowFailure`, `WorkflowSessionFailure`, `WorkflowDocumentSlot`, `DeepIDVError.apiCode` and `APIErrorCode`.
  - Resume a session: `resumeWorkflowSession(sessionID:)` and `DeepIDVWorkflowView(client:resumingSessionID:onResult:)`.
  - **Breaking:** `failureReason: String?` removed; use `failure: WorkflowFailure?`.
  - Public enums are non-exhaustive: `switch` needs `@unknown default`.
  - Targeted retries and SDK-written error text in workflows.
</Update>
