title: Troubleshooting
description: "Fixes for common iOS SDK problems: the camera won't open, auto-capture never fires, the document isn't recognized, sessions fail with conflict, and more."
---

Each entry starts with what you see.

## The app closes when the camera opens

**Cause:** `NSCameraUsageDescription` is missing from your `Info.plist`. iOS ends the app without it.
**Fix:** add the key. See [Installation](/integrate/sdks/ios/installation#declare-camera-access).

## "Camera access is off"

**Cause:** the user denied camera access, or it's restricted by Screen Time or device management.
**Fix:** the screen's **Open Settings** button takes the user there. Your app also receives `.failure` with kind `.cameraPermissionDenied`.

## The camera is black

**Cause:** you're on the iOS Simulator, which has no camera, or another app or view holds the camera.
**Fix:** test on a real device. Present only one SDK camera view at a time.

## Auto-capture never fires

**Causes and fixes:**
- **Glare or low light.** The message says **Avoid the glare**, or the frame never turns green. Move to even light.
- **The card is too small in the frame.** **Move a little closer**. On a tablet, the user may need to hold the card closer to the screen.
- **Movement.** The bar resets if the card moves. Rest the card or the phone on something.
- **The wrong camera.** A phone using `.front` asks the user to hold the card up to the selfie camera. Use `.back` on phones. See [Camera](/integrate/sdks/ios/camera).

The manual shutter appears after 8 seconds with a card in view, or 15 seconds at most, so the user is never stuck.

## The document's name never appears on the frame

**Cause:** document recognition isn't reaching deepidv. Capture still works with on-device checks.
**Fixes:**
- If you use [proxy mode](/integrate/sdks/ios/proxy-mode), forward `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/id-capture/detect`.
- Check `usesDocumentClassifier` isn't `false` in your config.
- Recognition runs only in workflows, not in `DocumentScannerView`.

## "Turn your license sideways" on an iPad held correctly

**Cause:** the iPad was turned during the scan.
**Fix:** lock the orientation on kiosks and fixed tablets. Reopen the view after turning the device.

## `.conflict` when presenting a workflow

| `error.apiCode` | Meaning | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `.sessionAlreadyStarted` | `sessionID:` was given a session that already started. | Use `resumingSessionID:`. |
| `.sessionTerminal` | The session is finished (submitted, failed or expired). | Read the outcome with `fetchWorkflowState`, or create a new session. |
| `.stepOutOfOrder` | `CustomFaceLivenessView` ran before the session reached its liveness step. | Run the earlier steps first. |

## `.validation` before the camera opens

**Cause:** the workflow contains a step the SDK can't run. Supported steps are ID verification and face liveness.
**Fix:** run the other steps elsewhere, or use a workflow with only these steps.

## `.authentication` or `.authorization`

**Cause:** the key or token in `apiKey` was rejected, by deepidv or by your proxy.
**Fix:** in proxy mode, check your proxy accepts the user's token and adds the deepidv key. Never ship the deepidv key in the app.

## `.insufficientFunds`

**Cause:** your organization's balance can't cover the check.
**Fix:** top up in the Admin Console.

## Re-verification says `.userNotFound` or `.notPreviouslyVerified`

See [Not eligible reasons](/integrate/sdks/ios/re-verification#noteligiblereason). Most often the email doesn't match the email of the verified session, or the user was verified on a different workflow.

## Contacting support

Send [support](mailto:support@deepidv.com):

- the SDK version and iOS version, and the device model;
- the session ID or re-verification ID;
- `error.kind`, `error.apiCode` and `error.code`;
- what the user saw, and roughly when.

<Warning>
  Don't send API keys, user tokens, or photos of anyone's ID or face. The session ID is enough for us to find the details.
</Warning>
